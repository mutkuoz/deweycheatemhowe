Built on trust. Specifically, yours.

For nearly a century, Dewey, Cheatem & Howe has stood beside its clients through mergers, disputes, inheritances and investigations. We have argued before every federal circuit, most state supreme courts, and one parole board rather more often than we would like.

Today the firm has 412 lawyers2 in four offices, organized around a single conviction: every client deserves to be heard, and every hour deserves to be billed.

We don’t promise outcomes. We promise attention: to your matter, to your documents, and to the precise moment you stop reading them.

Frequently asked questions

The only straight answers on this website.

What does the name Dewey, Cheatem & Howe mean?

It’s the surnames of our three founding partners. Any resemblance to the phrase “Do we cheat ’em? And how!” is a coincidence the firm is prepared to argue at length, and at partner rates.

How does Dewey, Cheatem & Howe bill its time?

In six-minute increments, rounded to the nearest hour. Partner time is $1,450 an hour, the initial consultation is complimentary, and the walk from reception to the conference room is billed at the partner rate.

Who are the firm’s name partners?

Franklin T. Dewey III leads litigation, Margaret Cheatem is managing partner and the only member of the Billing Committee, and Howard Howe handles client relations, including most questions that begin with his name.

Which clients has the firm represented?

Clients who have spoken about us include The Devil, Darth Vader, Tom Marvolo Riddle and J. Oswald White, alongside corporations, families and the occasional head of state. Most prefer to keep a low profile.

Where are the firm’s offices?

New York, our headquarters since 1931, plus London, Grand Cayman and Wilmington, Delaware. The Delaware office is a mailbox, and the Grand Cayman office is not currently taking appointments.
